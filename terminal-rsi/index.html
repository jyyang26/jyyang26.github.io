FDR → SPR

Progress matters.
Completion matters more.

Early: normalize semantic checks by task family.
Later: prioritize strict success over partial progress.

FDR · Family Dense Reward
R_{\mathrm{FDR}} = \begin{cases} \max\!\left(\dfrac{P}{D_f},0\right), & \text{valid report}, \\ S, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases}
Family-normalized progress · D_f > 0
SPR · Success-Prioritized Reward
R_{\mathrm{SPR}} = \begin{cases} S + \dfrac{P}{P + D'_f}, & \text{valid report}, \\ S, & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases}
Strict success + saturating progress · D'_f > 0

P: passed semantic checks; S \in \{0,1\}: strict terminal success. D_f: family calibration divisor; D'_f: recalibrated divisor. Both divisors are frozen within each phase.
